Seattle Traffic Collision Analysis ↗

An analysis of 221,266 SDOT collision records (2004–present) from Seattle Open Data, asking where the city should spend its safety budget. I built a weighted severity score to rank locations by outcome rather than raw crash volume, which surfaced cyclist-strike hotspots along the Burke-Gilman Trail — and ruled out missing streetlights as a cause, where the raw numbers first suggested one.

  • pandas
  • Matplotlib
  • Open Data
